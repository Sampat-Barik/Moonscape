# Data Attribution and Scientific Provenance — Moonscape

Moonscape utilizes authentic lunar remote sensing datasets published by NASA and international mission science teams.

---

## 1. Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) — Altimetry
- **Instrument:** Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA)
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. David E. Smith (MIT / NASA GSFC)
- **Dataset Curator:** Dr. Erwan Mazarico & Dr. Michael K. Barker (NASA GSFC Planetary Geology, Geophysics and Geochemistry Laboratory)
- **Archive:** NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) Geosciences Node / Planetary Geodynamics Data Archive (PGDA)
- **Key Publications:**
  - Barker, M.K., Mazarico, E., Neumann, G.A., Zuber, M.T., Haruyama, J., & Smith, D.E. (2021). *Improved LOLA elevation maps for south pole landing sites: Error estimates and their impact on illumination conditions.* **Planetary and Space Science**, 203, 105119. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2020.105119
  - Smith, D.E., Zuber, M.T., Jackson, G.B., et al. (2010). *The Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter Investigation on the Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Mission.* **Space Science Reviews**, 150(1-4), 209-241. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-009-9512-y

## 2. LRO — Thermal Radiometry
- **Instrument:** Diviner Lunar Radiometer Experiment (DLRE)
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. David A. Paige (University of California, Los Angeles)
- **Archive:** NASA PDS Geosciences Node / UCLA Diviner Data Portal
- **Key Publications:**
  - Paige, D.A., Foote, M.C., Greenhagen, B.T., et al. (2010). *The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Diviner Lunar Radiometer Experiment.* **Space Science Reviews**, 150(1-4), 125-160. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-009-9529-2
  - Williams, J.-P., Paige, D.A., Greenhagen, B.T., & Sefton-Nash, E. (2017). *The global surface temperatures of the Moon as measured by the Diviner Lunar Radiometer Experiment.* **Icarus**, 283, 300-325. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2016.08.012

---

## 3. LRO — Imaging
- **Instrument:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Wide Angle Camera (WAC) & Narrow Angle Camera (NAC)
- **Principal Investigator:** Dr. Mark S. Robinson (Arizona State University)
- **Archive:** NASA Planetary Data System Imaging Node / ASU LROC Archive
- **Key Publications:**
  - Robinson, M.S., Brylow, S.M., Tschimmel, M., et al. (2010). *Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC) Instrument Overview.* **Space Science Reviews**, 150(1-4), 81-124. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11214-010-9634-2
